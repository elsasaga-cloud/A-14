**Company Profile: Shenzhen Olycom Technology Co., Ltd.**  

**Overview**  
Shenzhen Olycom Technology Co., Ltd., established in 2003, is a leading Chinese manufacturer and provider of fiber optic communication and industrial networking solutions. With nearly two decades of experience, Olycom specializes in high-performance equipment for data transmission, industrial networking, and optical communications. The company’s products are widely used in various industries, including telecommunications, industrial automation, video surveillance, transportation, and smart city projects.  

Olycom’s extensive product portfolio includes **industrial Ethernet switches**, **PoE switches**, fiber optic converters, and advanced **Dense Wavelength Division Multiplexing (DWDM)** devices. With ISO9001-certified facilities and a strong commitment to innovation and quality, Olycom serves customers in over 120 countries worldwide.  

---

**Industrial Ethernet Switches**  
Olycom’s industrial switches are designed to withstand harsh environments and ensure reliable, uninterrupted connectivity. These switches are ideal for industrial automation, outdoor deployments, and mission-critical networks. Key features include:  
- **Rugged Design**: Industrial-grade construction with IP-rated enclosures to operate under extreme temperatures, vibrations, and humidity.  
- **Redundant Power Supply**: Support for wide voltage inputs (12V-48V DC) with redundancy to ensure power reliability.  
- **High Performance**: Options include **managed and unmanaged industrial Ethernet switches** supporting gigabit and fast Ethernet, providing real-time data transmission for industrial networks.  
- **Enhanced Security**: Managed switches incorporate advanced features such as VLAN, QoS, and network redundancy protocols (RSTP, MSTP, ERPS) for enhanced stability.  

Olycom’s industrial switches are widely deployed in manufacturing plants, transportation networks, power utilities, and IoT-based smart infrastructures.  

---

**DWDM Equipment**  
Olycom’s **DWDM (Dense Wavelength Division Multiplexing)** devices are designed to maximize the capacity of optical fiber networks by enabling the simultaneous transmission of multiple data channels over a single fiber. These devices are critical for building high-capacity and scalable communication networks. Key features include:  
- **High Channel Capacity**: Support for multiple wavelengths (up to 96 channels) to optimize fiber utilization.  
- **Low Signal Loss**: Precision engineering ensures minimal signal attenuation for long-distance data transmission.  
- **Versatile Devices**: Olycom offers **DWDM multiplexers, demultiplexers**, and **optical add-drop multiplexers (OADMs)** for flexible network configurations.  
- **Scalability**: DWDM solutions are easily scalable, making them ideal for expanding metro and long-haul networks.  
- **Cost-Efficient**: By increasing the capacity of existing fiber infrastructure, Olycom’s DWDM equipment minimizes the need for additional physical fibers, reducing operational costs.  

These solutions are widely adopted by telecom operators, data centers, and enterprises seeking to enhance their optical network performance and bandwidth capacity.  

---

**Commitment to Quality and Global Reach**  
Olycom Technology combines cutting-edge technology with rigorous quality control to deliver products that meet global standards. The company maintains a strong focus on research and development, ensuring its solutions remain at the forefront of fiber optic and networking technologies.  

With a growing presence in international markets, Shenzhen Olycom Technology Co., Ltd. continues to provide reliable, high-performance solutions for modern communication and industrial applications.
