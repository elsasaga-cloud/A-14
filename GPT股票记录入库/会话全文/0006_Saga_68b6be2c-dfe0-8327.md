可以，而且**非常适合做成 roadmap**。  
而且我要先给你一句很关键的话：

> **你这个东西，一旦用“roadmap”来表达，就彻底从“卖产品”升级成“陪客户走一段路”。**

这正是欧洲那些 10 来个人、能活 30–50 年的小公司最典型的表达方式。

下面我分三层来帮你 **确认可行性 → 优化结构 → 给你一版可直接用的表达模板**。

---

## 一、结论先行：你这个“roadmap”是成立的，而且是高质量的

但要注意一件事：

❌ 不是「我们的产品 roadmap」  
✅ 而是 **Customer Network Evolution Roadmap（以客户为主语）**

你现在脑子里的逻辑，本质是：

> **“客户网络怎么成长 → 我们怎么一路陪着”**

这非常高级，也非常“solution company”。

---

## 二、roadmap 的正确打开方式（非常重要）

### ❌ 不要这样做
- 年份 + 芯片 + 型号  
- “2026 引入 Centec / 2027 引入 DWDM”

这会把你拉回 **vendor 视角**。

---

### ✅ 应该这样做
用 **阶段（Phase）+ 能力（Capability）+ 客户收益（Value）**

---

## 三、推荐的 Roadmap 总体结构（你可以直接照这个来）

### Title（建议）

**Managed Networking Solutions Roadmap**  
*A scalable network evolution path from Access to Core*

或者更“陪伴型”一点：

**A Long-Term Networking Roadmap — From Access to Core**

---

## Phase 1 – Access Foundation  
**Reliable, manageable access networks**

**What this stage solves**
- Stable industrial / enterprise access
- Managed switching, PoE, redundancy
- Local customization and long-term supply

**Our focus**
- Access layer managed switches  
- Marvell-based platforms  
- Tailored software & documentation  
- Long lifecycle & continuous supply

📌 *This is where most customer relationships start.*

> 这一句非常重要：  
> 👉 客户会自动把你定位成“起点”。

---

## Phase 2 – Aggregation Expansion  
**Scalability without architectural disruption**

**Why customers reach this stage**
- Traffic growth
- System integration complexity
- Higher availability requirements

**Our approach**
- Architectural continuity from Access
- Unified management logic
- Ecosystem compatibility (optics, DAC, modules)

**Technology strategy**
- Carefully selected aggregation platforms  
- Evaluated for stability, maturity and roadmap alignment

📌 这里你已经开始**暗示 Centec，但不点名**。

---

## Phase 3 – Core Integration  
**Performance, stability and long-term operability**

**Customer needs at this stage**
- High throughput
- Predictable behavior
- Minimal vendor switching risk

**Our role**
- Provide core-ready switching platforms
- Maintain consistent operational experience
- Support long-term system planning

**Technology selection principle**
- Stability over novelty  
- Ecosystem maturity over specs  
- Long-term supply over short-term advantage  

👉 **在这里再轻描淡写一句：**

> Based on this philosophy, CENTEC-based solutions are introduced as part of our core portfolio.

**Prepared, not rushed**
