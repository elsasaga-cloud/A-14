Workflow

Onboard (Sky End): The TA510-GE-X is connected to the drone’s camera or payload via Ethernet. It converts the video/data signal into light and sends it down the fiber
Fiber Link: The signal travels through the Kevlar-reinforced fiber optic tether
Ground (Base End): A matching TA510-GE-X converts the light signal back to Ethernet, which is then fed into a monitor, recorder, or ground control station




Port

1*Fiber + 1*Ethernet

Ideal to use SFP or Fixed Transceiver: flexible communication distances and optical connectors
Ethernet speed is 10/100Mbps or 10/100/1000Mbps auto-adaptive: sufficient for compressed 4K video (H.264/H.265) used in drones
